        The big picture

        Locke does not identify the state of nature with war. He thinks it begins with a real moral law and means of enforcement, but that private enforcement becomes unreliable when disputes and scarcity grow.

        1. Natural equality and moral duties make peaceful life possible.
        2. Everyone may enforce the Law of Nature because no one is naturally superior.
        3. Private judgment, retaliation, and disagreement make that arrangement insecure.
        4. Money creates scarcity and multiplies disputes.
        5. Civil government supplies known laws, an impartial judge, and dependable enforcement.
