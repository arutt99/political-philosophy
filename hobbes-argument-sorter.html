        The Big Picture

        The entire argument can be reduced to this movement:

        1. 1Endless desires
        2. 2Need for power
        3. 3Mutual vulnerability
        4. 4Competition
        5. 5Suspicion
        6. 6Preemptive violence
        7. 7State of war
        8. 8Recognition that peace is better
        9. 9Inability to trust agreements
        10. 10Need for an enforcing sovereign

        The most important insight is that Hobbes is not simply saying “people are bad, therefore we need government.” His stronger argument is that even people who prefer peace can end up in conflict when they are vulnerable, uncertain about one another, and unable to enforce agreements. Government solves that problem by creating reliable expectations about how others will behave.
