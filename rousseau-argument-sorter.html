        The big picture

        Rousseau rejects the idea that war belongs to the original state of nature. Natural people are independent, limited in their desires, and restrained by pity; conflict grows only as social life, comparison, property, and inequality transform them.

        1. Natural humans are solitary, self-sufficient, and have few needs.
        2. Self-preservation is restrained by pity, while social morality and rights do not yet exist.
        3. Perfectibility produces tools, cooperation, families, and new wants.
        4. Comparison, dependence, property, and inequality turn these innovations into conflict.
        5. The rich promote laws and government that secure property while fixing inequality in place.
